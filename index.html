Clock

EU server time is this zone's wall clock. After 25 October the local hour stays the same. Daily reset is 08:00. Etc/GMT-2 is fixed UTC+2. Use it only if the in-game clock still shows summer time. The sign is inverted.

Paste spawn

Copy a line, then press Paste spawn. The clock is the server zone. A copied aion2timers row works too. Loot and the Korean name are ignored. The clock after Spawns in is used, not the countdown.

  • Gartua 00:34
  • Immortal Gartua Spawns at 6:43 PM

A later time is today. An earlier time is tomorrow. A matching field boss keeps its interval. An unknown name is added. A clock timer is left alone.

Field bosses

Field bosses start off. Turn on overlay or alarm for the ones you want. That choice stays across updates. Settings, Catalog, Altgard or Verteron hides the other faction. Fixed world bosses stay on their own timers and are not listed again as field bosses. Argo, the Spirit King in the Abyss is a field boss. A down report shows a timer. Refresh shows Updating, then Current and the last updated time. Field bosses can refresh every few minutes. A boss reported up keeps its last spawn time. Also alert when it starts sounds again at the start. A timer can turn that off in the editor. The Edge widget Yustiel button shows Yustiel field bosses for the PvP rift. Press it again for Marchutan.
